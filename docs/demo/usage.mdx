# Usage

What you can do in the Demo.

<details>
<summary>Windows:</summary>

- Drag the empty part of the top bar to move the window
- Release at the left, right or top screen edge to snap the window to the left or the right half of the screen, depending on which half the pointer is in. A translucent preview shows where it will land
- Double click the empty part of the top bar, or use the maximize button, to make it full screen
- Drag an edge or a corner to resize. A window cannot become narrower than its top bar needs, so the tabs and buttons always fit
- Use the top bar buttons to snap, maximize, minimize into the dock and close
- Drag the insert button of the top bar over another window to merge all the tabs of the first one into it

</details>

<details>
<summary>Tabs:</summary>

- Click to change to the tab
- Hold Ctrl and click to show it next to the tabs that are already visible (tiling)
- Double click to close
- Drag to swap places in the same window
- Drag out to an empty place of the desktop to create a new window (the only tab of a window snaps back)
- Drag out and drop on another window's top bar to add it there and show it
- Drag out and drop on another window's content to add it there without showing it
- Click **+** to open the **New Tab** picker. Choosing an app turns the tab into that app. Hold Ctrl while clicking **+** to keep the other tabs visible

</details>

<details>
<summary>Dock:</summary>

- Click an app icon to open the app in the active workspace
- Every open tab of an app has an underline below its icon
- Click an underline to bring that window forward and open its tab, switching to its workspace if needed
- Ctrl+click an underline to minimize or restore the window
- Hover an underline to preview the window: it is raised above the others and shown even if minimized
- Drag an icon to reorder it. Drag a whole section of the dock to reorder the sections
- Hold Shift and scroll over the clock and player section to switch between the clock and the mini player

</details>

<details>
<summary>Start menu:</summary>

- Click the logo in the dock to open or close it
- Press Escape or click outside to close it
- Click a pinned app to open it

</details>

<details>
<summary>Workspaces:</summary>

- There are four workspaces, each with its own windows
- A new window opens in the workspace that is active
- Press **Left Alt + W** to slide the workspace switcher in from the top of the screen, and press it again to hide it
- Click a number to switch

</details>

<details>
<summary>Language:</summary>

- The language is taken from your last choice, then from the browser, then English
- Press **Left Alt + L** to slide the language picker in from the top right corner, and press it again to hide it
- Your choice is remembered

</details>

<details>
<summary>Context menu:</summary>

- Right click shows a menu. Apps can have their own items
- Double right click in the same place shows the browser's own menu instead

</details>

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Keys | Action |
| ---- | ------ |
| Left Alt + W | Show or hide the workspace switcher |
| Left Alt + L | Show or hide the language picker |
| Escape | Close the start menu |
| Ctrl + click on a tab | Show the tab next to the visible ones |
| Ctrl + click on a dock underline | Minimize or restore the window |
| Shift + mouse wheel over the dock's clock | Switch the widget |
